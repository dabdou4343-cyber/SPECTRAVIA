FTIR Analyzer

PDF spectrum analysis · Prototype

1. Import PDF

Choose a PDF containing an FTIR spectrum. The PDF is processed locally in your browser.

2. Select the plot area

After the PDF page appears, drag on the graph to select a tight rectangle around the spectrum trace. Avoid axis labels and captions.

3. Calibrate X axis

Enter the wavenumber at the left and right edges of your selected plot. FTIR axes commonly decrease from left to right (for example, 4000 to 400 cm⁻¹).

4. Results

The detected positions are estimates from the selected plot image. Review the marked peaks and confirm the functional-group assignments with chemistry references and the full spectrum.

Scientific limitations

This prototype digitizes a rasterized plot after manual region selection and axis calibration. Grid lines, overlapping bands, low-resolution scans, and colored traces can affect detection. Functional-group ranges overlap; assignments are suggestions, not definitive identification. For validated quantitative analysis, use instrument-exported numeric data.
